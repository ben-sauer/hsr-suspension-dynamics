# References
Aparna, A, et al. Dynamics of High-Speed Railways ME5670: Vehicle Dynamics Presentation Group B.
- Guidance for formulating equations of motion.<br>
Fu, Bin, et al. “Active Suspension in Railway Vehicles: A Literature Survey.” Railway Engineering Science, vol. 28, no. 1, Mar. 2020, pp. 3–35, https://doi.org/10.1007/s40534-020-00207-w.
- General introductory guide on railway suspension, disturbances, and in-depth explanation of active suspension control methods. Also describes the optimization goals considered when improving rail.<br>
Goicolea, José. Simplified Mechanical Description of AVE S-103 -ICE3 Velaro E High Speed Train. 2014.
- Reference for dimension and mass assumptions. <br>
Yoshimura, T., et al. “An Active Suspension Model for Rail/Vehicle Systems with Preview and Stochastic Optimal Control.” Journal of Sound and Vibration, vol. 166, no. 3, Sept. 1993, pp. 507–519, https://doi.org/10.1006/jsvi.1993.1309. Accessed 31 May 2019.
- Active suspension model is proposed for rail systems. Model is simplified to three degrees of freedom as axle and rail stiffness is assumed to be significantly larger than stiffness between body and axles. Bounce of body, roll of body, and pitch of body are considered. Model provides guidance for governing equation parameters. <br>
Younesian, Davood, and Amir Nankali. “Spectral Optimization of High-Speed Train Suspension Systems.” International Journal of Vehicle Structures and Systems, vol. 1, no. 4, 17 Nov. 2009, https://doi.org/10.4273/ijvss.1.4.06. Accessed 23 Feb. 2020.
- Introduction to active-suspension systems of high speed rail. Spectral model uses multiple degrees of freedom, and seeks to optimize the trade-off between better ride quality and longer fatigue life. The wheel to rail contact is modeled as a linear Hertzian contact spring. <br>
Zhang, Heng, et al. “An Active Suspension System for Enhancing Running Safety of High-Speed Trains under Strong Crosswind.” Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers, Part F: Journal of Rail and Rapid Transit, vol. 0, no. 1-15, 14 Oct. 2023, https://doi.org/10.1177/09544097231206216. Accessed 15 Jan. 2024.
- Describes effects of cross-wind disturbance on train dynamics. Wind generally affects lateral, yaw, and pitch stability.
